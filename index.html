Yash Vadi

I am a Research Engineer on the AI Search team at ServiceNow in Toronto. My work focuses on improving the search relevancy by evaluating differnt retrivel, reranking methods.

Previously, I worked in Multimodal and Document AI team at ServiceNow, for evaluating and improving the document extraction flows. I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) at the Université de Montréal, where I was affiliated with Mila. My interests include machine learning, NLP, information retrieval, computer vision, and multimodal learning.
