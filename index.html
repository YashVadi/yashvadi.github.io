Yash Vadi

I am a Research Engineer on the AI Search team at ServiceNow in Toronto. My work focuses on data curation, relevance evaluation, and ranking for enterprise search.

Previously, I worked on document intelligence at ServiceNow Research, including invoice information extraction, long-table extraction, layout-aware synthetic data, and PII redaction. I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) at the Université de Montréal, where I was affiliated with Mila. My interests include machine learning, NLP, information retrieval, computer vision, and multimodal learning.
